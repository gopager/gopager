### E-18 Security review of the fallback budget change

Date: 2026-11-10, same environment. The automated security review of `312ab25` found that suspending the 64 KiB check while a fallback alternative is tried left one render without an early bound: up to 256 blocks interpolating large values (for example 256 KiB results) could build tens of MiB. Valid; the suspension (`trying`) is replaced by two bounds that always apply: output with content (label, title, URL or text that is not empty without control characters) counts against the 64 KiB output bound as before, and everything the rendered items hold, with or without content, counts against a hard 1 MiB bound (`maxHeldLayoutBytes`); a discarded alternative gives both back, and the serializer's exact final check stays.
