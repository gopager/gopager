## Implemented behavior (specified in `docs/`)

- Atomic append in the caller's transaction (no second connection, no post-commit relay, no callback replay, `COMMIT_UNCERTAIN` resolved through the operation's receipt), the sequenced `domain_events` log with `(tx_id, seq)` gap-safe reads, `consumer_offsets`, the `NOTIFY` wake-up hint, the envelope fields and compatibility rules, producer domains and the event type table: [Architecture: Domain events and outbox](../../docs/architecture.md#domain-events-and-outbox) and [Transactions and the store abstraction](../../docs/architecture.md#transactions-and-the-store-abstraction). Typed producers: [`internal/events`](../../internal/events/) (identity, master-data, configuration, intake, correction and Processing facts); the append adapter is [`eventstore`](../../internal/persistence/ent/eventstore/). Facts contain no names, settings, credentials or Payload.
- Intake facts (Gateway state changes only on effective state change, rejection, implausible Observation time, ambiguous Network with the chosen Network and resolution, Message membership, one `assignment.changed` per Transmission with the ordered resolutions or the changed targets with before and after, plus `previousNetworkId` for a Gateway mapping move, [D-141](../decisions/03-assignment-and-correction.md#d-141)): [Architecture: Intake](../../docs/architecture.md#intake-one-transaction) and [Historical correction](../../docs/architecture.md#historical-correction-worker-role). Suppression facts carry the suppression ID and revision, target position, Network, optional Unit and `created` or `lifted`.
- Processing work: candidate admission and reselection, `SKIP LOCKED` claims with `NOTIFY` wake-up, lease, renewal and fencing, the dispatcher bounds, the five automatic attempts with the 30 s, 2 min, 10 min and 30 min delays and plus or minus 20 % jitter, terminal `failed`, authorized manual retry and `processing.completed` only for a published selected result: [Architecture: Processing](../../docs/architecture.md#processing-worker-role), [Workflows: Retries](../../docs/workflows.md#retries), [Worker dispatch](../../docs/workflows.md#worker-dispatch), [Publication](../../docs/workflows.md#publication) and [Events](../../docs/workflows.md#events), and the retry table of [Plugins](../../docs/plugins.md#failure-classification-and-retry).
- Correction work: [Architecture: Historical correction](../../docs/architecture.md#historical-correction-worker-role). Correction job states are not public Domain events.
- Gateway contact-loss monitor (worker role, database clock, `SKIP LOCKED` scan, fencing by re-checked condition) and one `gateway.state-changed` fact plus history row per Gateway revision: [Architecture: Gateway state and contact loss](../../docs/architecture.md#gateway-state-and-contact-loss-worker-role); flags and retry logging: [Operations](../../docs/operations.md#configuration-reference).
- Failure and uncertainty classification across a transaction: [Architecture: Errors and failure codes](../../docs/architecture.md#errors-and-failure-codes).

- Consumer framework and live consumption (AP-10): `events.WakeHub`, `events.Consumer`, `events.Runner` (batch transaction, offset atomic with the effect, blocking on unknown versions, at-least-once with deduplication by Domain event ID plus consumer name, `--event-poll-interval`, lifecycle in `cmd/gopager/events_runtime.go`): [Architecture: Domain events and outbox](../../docs/architecture.md#domain-events-and-outbox); live streams with client-held positions and the replay window (`--live-replay-window`; retention keeps facts at least that long): [Architecture: Query](../../docs/architecture.md#query-api-role), [HTTP API: Messages: live stream](../../docs/http-api.md#messages-live-stream), [Operations](../../docs/operations.md#configuration-reference). Message-related facts carry the Message time ([D-144](../decisions/05-events-and-delivery.md#d-144)).

Decisions that remain binding: state, required fact and work are atomic in the primary store; the outbox state is the truth and a wake-up is only an optimization; a fact is never delivered as committed before commit; recording a fact does not replace current consumer or Delivery authorization and does not grant raw Payload to a browser, plugin or SSE consumer; consumers commit their effect and their offset or receipt together and deduplicate by Domain event ID plus consumer identity; internal restart may duplicate processing and needs idempotent consumers; provider I/O keeps C-09 uncertainty and is not exactly-once because of a job lease; a Transmission's intake and Grouping facts are published on commit while Processing may still run, and result-dependent reactions subscribe to `processing.completed`; manual restoration of older store state is the operator's responsibility ([Operations](../../docs/operations.md#backup-and-restore-responsibility)).

## Planned and not yet implemented

- **Generic job lifecycle.** `ClaimJob(context, role, workerRef)`, `CompleteJob(leaseRef, result)` and `RescheduleJob(leaseRef, classifiedFailure)` as one shared mechanism are not built; Processing and correction each claim their own rows. Where a new job family (Delivery, retention, cleanup) needs it, it uses a short primary transaction that checks the role, increments the fencing token and sets a lease deadline (see [shared conventions](shared-conventions.md#ap-02-common-schemas-and-limits) for the lease numbers); renew, complete and reschedule require the matching token and an unexpired lease; a lost worker cannot publish stale results; expiry before an external start permits reclaim, while expiry after a recorded external start produces `uncertain` unless a provider idempotency proof shows safe recovery; no SQL transaction is held across plugin or provider I/O. There is no mandatory dead-letter broker: failed and uncertain primary-store records are the inspection and recovery queue.
- **Replay and retention.** Automatic fan-out accepts Domain events for at most 24 h from `recordedAt` and only within the current subscription start, revision and age rules, with no automatic backfill on subscribing; pending unconsumed facts are never silently pruned, expiry marks dependent work expired and inspectable. Terminal consumer and Delivery deduplication receipts are kept until no automatic replay or active job can use them (at least 24 h after the terminal state and past the replay deadline); compact receipts may remain, secret Payload is not retained indefinitely. Retention of `domain_events`, `consumer_offsets` and receipts joins the C-12 list and is not implemented ([Operations: Event Log Retention](../../docs/operations.md#event-log-retention)). Manual replay or reprocessing suppresses external reactions by default through `replayMode`: AP-11 owns the vocabulary (`normal`, `reprocess`) and suppresses automatic Delivery fan-out for every non-`normal` mode (its tests synthesize such facts); AP-13 Reprocess publishes `processing.completed` with `reprocess`; deliberate redelivery is a fresh confirmed operation.
- **Plugin events.** `plugin.<plugin-id>.<type>` facts of a declared plugin carry a manifest schema, an explicit data classification and Principal context, bounded data and causation; internal publication needs a declared SDK capability (C-10, REQ-139: design compatibility only).
- **Subscription and Delivery facts (AP-11).** Subscription create, update, pause and delete emit `configuration.changed` with aggregate `subscription`. Delivery attempts emit no facts; their status is read with `delivery.read`.
- **Remaining fact vocabulary.** Delivery, Query and account-lifecycle facts (`producer.domain` `delivery` and `query` are reserved and produce nothing yet; Delivery facts are REQ-139 later).
