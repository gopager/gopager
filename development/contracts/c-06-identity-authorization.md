## Implemented behavior (specified in `docs/` and OpenAPI)

- Credential selection (an `Authorization` header wins, then the session cookie, then the guest; no fallback after a bad credential), guest read-only rule, early credential check before the body, uniform expiry: [HTTP API: Authentication](../../docs/http-api.md#authentication). Routes and schemas: [OpenAPI v1](../../api/openapi/v1.yaml) (`info.version` 1.7.0), tag `Auth` and the security schemes.
- Login (NFC plus case-folded username comparison, generic rejection, session lifetime, cookie attributes), Origin and CSRF policy, login rate limits, public Origin and full HTTP support (no required TLS; the `Secure` cookie attribute follows the public Origin): [HTTP API: Sessions](../../docs/http-api.md#sessions-browser-login), [Origin and CSRF policy](../../docs/http-api.md#origin-and-csrf-policy), [Login rate limiting](../../docs/http-api.md#login-rate-limiting).
- Gateway Bearer credentials (format, hash-only storage, one-time display, one pending replacement with first-use cutover, revoke, no expiry, administrative channel and rights): [HTTP API: Gateway authentication](../../docs/http-api.md#gateway-authentication) and [Gateway administration](../../docs/http-api.md#gateway-administration). The credential authenticates exactly one Gateway and grants nothing beyond its assignments.
- Single-Gateway delegation (REQ-134): the `gateway` scope kind, `gateway.diagnostics.read` and `gateway.credential.rotate`, evaluated per assigned Agency with the Gateway as target and stopping when the Gateway is deleted: [HTTP API: Authorization](../../docs/http-api.md#authorization) (scope table and "Gateway delegation"). `authz.ReachableAgencies` is the central list-filter decision for Networks and Gateways.
- Grants, the action and scope-kind catalog, additive union, read and write scope separation, restricting access keys, maximum age (inclusive, per Unit appearance), the two-step data-then-field check, global field permissions, delegation, password policy and per-resource scope evaluation (including master-data, template, Organization-deletion and correction rights): [HTTP API: Authorization](../../docs/http-api.md#authorization). Explicit bootstrap of the first account and its actions and fields: [Operations: First Start and Bootstrap](../../docs/operations.md#first-start-and-bootstrap). Point authorization, native filtering and locking: [Architecture: Authorization](../../docs/architecture.md#authorization). Typed code: [`internal/authz`](../../internal/authz/) and [`internal/identity`](../../internal/identity/).

- Query fields, saved-view rights (`saved-view.manage-global`, scope `all`; personal views for non-guest `message.read` holders; global views readable by Message readers and their managers) and search admission (REQ-108, REQ-133..138, AP-10): [HTTP API: Messages: query and detail](../../docs/http-api.md#messages-query-and-detail) (field-group table), [Saved views](../../docs/http-api.md#saved-views), [Search rate limiting](../../docs/http-api.md#search-rate-limiting), [Authorization](../../docs/http-api.md#authorization).

Decisions that remain binding: a stable internal Principal is separate from its credentials; matching email establishes neither identity nor account linkage; authentication is not authorization; unknown actions, fields and scopes are invalid, not allowed; a denied result never explains another account, target or grant; display-language selection changes no authorization outcome; data already handed off is not recallable; plugin and processing fields are not implicitly readable; field visibility has no data scope (D-69), while row eligibility keeps its scope and age; a Gateway submits only through its own assigned Network credential; the API-key owner attribution is the owner plus the nonsecret key ID.

## Planned and not yet implemented

- **Management of identity records over HTTP (AP-14).** `CreateAccount`, `ChangeProfile`, `SetAccountState`, `ChangeGrants`, `ChangeUserGroupMembership`, field-permission changes, `RevokeSession` and `RevokeKey` have no HTTP route yet. Each takes an expected revision and, for creating commands, an actor-bound `commandId`; each writes configuration, access revision, audit and the invalidation fact atomically; secret-bearing responses show the secret once and a retry never creates a second effective credential. Account kind is `human | technical`, lifecycle `active | suspended | deleting | deleted`. Technical accounts never acquire an implicit human-login capability.
- **Access keys.** No endpoint issues them. Only the authorized owner creates personal keys; technical-account keys need the target permission. Default lifetime 90 days, operator-configurable including unlimited, with the requested expiry shown before issuance; self-rotation within the owner's rights with an optional overlap of at most 24 h; revocation and password-policy changes increment the access revision immediately; only the nonsecret ID, owner, restrictions, created, expiry, revocation and last-use times are stored.
- **Recovery (REQ-071, 073..075).** A manager issues a one-time recovery proof (lifetime 30 minutes) that the user consumes together with the correct username; consumption is atomic, single use and subject to the local-password capability and leaves a consumed receipt until expiry plus 24 h. Setting, changing and recovering a password never bypass the `password.local` exclusion; a change checks the old and confirmed new password and invalidates other Sessions and outstanding proofs. Administrators neither know nor set a user's new password. Recovery requests and automatic dispatch (REQ-072/076) are dropped under D-135, so a proof is handed over by the manager and never logged or exposed through Subscriptions or Delivery history. Local administrative rescue stays an explicit, audited path issuing a short-lived recovery proof, never a silent account replacement or permanent bypass.
- **Further throttles** (configurable, not capacity promises; login limits are in the HTTP API page): proof consumption 10/min per normalized username and 60/min per instance via the login-attempt bucket mechanism, generic error, `RATE_LIMITED` plus `Retry-After`, no issuance throttle; outbound starts 60 per minute per Subscriber plus the configured target and provider rate. Use bounded buckets and generic errors, keep unknown-account buckets identical, never use a shared location as an account key, and report `RATE_LIMITED` with a safe retry delay.
- **Subscription and Delivery scope resolution (AP-11, planning before coding).** Subscriptions: `subscription.manage-own` on the caller's own account (scope `all` or `account`), `subscription.manage-global` on the technical subscriber's account. Deliveries: `delivery.read | retry | redeliver` match a Delivery through the subscriber's account (`account`), any Agency, Unit or Unit collection of the Delivery's recorded context, or `all`; grant ages apply to the Delivery's age anchor. `authz.DeliveryAccess` compiles these grants for list filtering in SQL before paging, `authz.Authorize` decides point reads and commands. Sending admission (`--delivery-sends-per-minute`, `--delivery-target-sends-per-minute`): [C-09](c-09-delivery.md#ap-11-core-binding-planning-before-coding).
- **Remaining field-group review (D-68).** The memberships of Protocol, form and subtype (`payload`) and of the resolved Network ID and name (`gateway`) are implemented as source-context fields. Still pending: the exact receipt subfield membership of the `gateway` group and the separation of source identity from receipt details; saved-view behavior with unavailable fields is implemented (`unavailableFilters`). No new group or grant predicate is added until an owner amendment.
- **Issuing single-Gateway grants (AP-14).** The `gateway` scope kind and the delegation actions are implemented (see the implemented section); creating such a grant over HTTP is part of grant management.
- **Preference action (AP-13).** `preference.manage-own` (scope `all`, self-service like `key.manage-own`) lets an account read and change its own preferences under revision checks.
- **OIDC and SCIM** bind to the same internal Principal ID and are specified in C-13.
- **Plugins** have no installation or internal-read action: installation is local operator authority, and plugins read Core data through the versioned HTTP API as their technical Principal ([C-10](c-10-plugin-host.md)).
- **Agency-bound definitions ([D-151](../decisions/06-identity-and-authorization.md#d-151), [AP-21](../work-packages/ap-21-attribute-schemas.md)).** New action `attribute-schema.manage` (scopes `all`, `agency`). Creating, changing, deleting or reassigning a Workflow, Layout or Attribute schema needs its management action (`workflow.*`, `presentation.manage`, `attribute-schema.manage`) for every assigned Agency, added and removed; a Workflow narrowed to Units may be managed with grants covering those Units. Workflow and Layout lists return definitions assigned to at least one Agency where the caller holds the matching read or use right. Attribute-schema metadata is not confidential: it is listed to callers holding `message.read`, `workflow.read`, `presentation.manage`, `subscription.manage-own`, `subscription.manage-global`, `saved-view.manage-global`, `unit.update` or `attribute-schema.manage` in at least one assigned Agency; the Agency filter only scopes choice lists.
- **Field groups ([D-150](../decisions/04-processing-and-plugins.md#d-150), [D-152](../decisions/07-query-and-live.md#d-152)).** Field groups stay fixed (D-68, D-69). The syntax `extension.<plugin>.<field>` is removed from `authz.ValidField`; Message attributes belong to `derived` plus the result's stored source groups, Unit attributes to `unit`.
- **AP-21 binding (planning before coding, 2026-11-11).** Implemented by AP-21 ([handover](../execution/ap-21.md#handover)). `attribute-schema.manage` joins the catalog with scopes `all` and `agency`. Workflow and Layout authorization follow the [C-11 AP-21 binding](c-11-processing-presentation.md#ap-21-binding-planning-before-coding). Attribute-schema lists and reads use `permissions.ReadScope` over the listed actions with this scope mapping: `all` sees every schema; `agency` the schemas assigned to that Agency; `unit` and `unitCollection` the schemas assigned to the Agency of those Units; `account` (the scope of `subscription.manage-own` and `subscription.manage-global`, which are not Agency-bound) every schema, because the metadata is not confidential. The query parameter `agency` narrows a list to one Agency for choice lists. Correction of the planned wording: `subscription.manage-own` and `subscription.manage-global` have no Agency scope, so Layout choice is bound to `presentation.manage` or `message.read` in an assigned Agency instead (C-11, C-09).
